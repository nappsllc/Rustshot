# line; {name} placeholders must stay as they are; plural strings have one
# line per CLDR form the language uses: key[zero|one|two|few|many|other];
# escapes \n (new line), \t, \s (a space at an edge), \\. Metadata:
# @name (the language in itself), @dir (ltr or rtl), @plural (the rule:
# see src/i18n/plural.rs). US spelling.
